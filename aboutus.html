About YMT

Financial planning with knowledge and care

YMT Development Resources is a financial planning specialist, licensed by Securities Commission of Malaysia, dedicated to providing individual and corporate financial planning solutions.

Our licensed Chartered Financial Consultants and Certified Financial Planners offer vast experience in helping individual families, businesses, and charities analyze their financial situations and find the best path to success. We prioritize knowledge and offer holistic financial planning tailored to each stage of life.
